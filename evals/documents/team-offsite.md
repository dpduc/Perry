# Team offsite

The team offsite is on October 21-22 at Lakeview Lodge in Annecy. Sam Patel is organising it.

Day 1 is the roadmap review. Day 2 is a guided hike around the lake, so bring hiking boots
and a rain jacket. The budget is 250 euros per person, covering the room and meals.
Trains from Lyon leave every two hours; we meet at the lodge at 10:00 on the first day.
