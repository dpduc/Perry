# Garden log 2026

- May 20: sowed basil between the tomatoes.
- June 3: aphids on the tomatoes. Sprayed neem oil twice, a week apart, and they cleared up.
- July 28: first tomato harvest, about two kilos.
- August 15: the basil bolted in the heat. Next year, sow it in partial shade.
